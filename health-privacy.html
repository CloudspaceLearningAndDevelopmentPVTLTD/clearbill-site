Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • On your device only: bill photos, OCR text, your name, address, date of birth, account numbers, insurance member ID, and any details you type in. This information never leaves your device.
  • On our servers: only de-identified billing data — procedure codes, descriptions with identity removed, dollar amounts, US state, bill type, and insurance status. This data cannot reasonably identify you.
  • Account data: an anonymous device session token; purchase receipts processed by the app store.

What we do NOT do

  • We never receive, collect, sell, or share data that identifies you.
  • We never use your information for advertising.
  • We never run analytics or tracking SDKs on screens containing health data.
  • We never use your data to train AI models.

Retention

  • Server-side de-identified audit data is deleted automatically within 30 days.
  • On-device data is under your control — use "Delete my data" in Settings to wipe everything instantly.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data at any time via Settings → Delete my data, or by contacting contact@cdandlc.com.

Consent

We collect consumer health data only with your explicit opt-in consent, shown at first launch. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your data.
