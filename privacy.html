Privacy Policy

ClearBill is designed so that your identity never reaches our servers.

On-device: bill images, OCR text, and your personal details are processed and stored only on your device, protected by iOS/Android encryption.

Server-side: de-identified line items only (codes, amounts, state). No names, DOB, SSN, addresses, account numbers, images, or free-text OCR are transmitted. Retained ≤30 days, then deleted.

Third parties: payment processing is handled entirely by Apple / Google. We use no advertising SDKs, no analytics on health-data screens, and sell no data.

Breach notification: in the event of a breach of identifiable health information, we will notify affected users, the FTC, and media where required by the Health Breach Notification Rule.
